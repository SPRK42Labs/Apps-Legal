Support

If you need help with Drive Simply, contact support@sprk42.com.

When contacting support

Please include the device model, operating-system version, Drive Simply app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to resolve the issue.

Driving and safety

Do not use or troubleshoot Drive Simply while driving. If an issue occurs during a drive, keep the phone secured or put away and deal with it only when safely parked. See Driving Safety.

Location and journey recording

Drive Simply relies on device location services during an explicitly started drive. GPS availability, permissions, battery-management settings and operating-system behaviour can affect distance and route-place recording.

Data and privacy

See the Privacy Policy and Delete Your Data pages for information about journal storage and deletion.

Purchases

Drive Simply Plus may be offered through Apple App Store or Google Play. When purchases are available, billing and subscription management will use the relevant store systems. If you have a purchase problem, contact us and include the platform and purchase date, but never send full payment-card details.
