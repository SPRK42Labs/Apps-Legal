Delete Your Drive Simply Data

Effective: 31 August 2026 · Version 1.0

The current version of Drive Simply does not require a Drive Simply account. Journal information is stored locally on your device.

Delete individual drive records

Where the app provides a delete option for a drive, you can remove that drive directly from Drive Simply. Some historical or integrity-protected records may be treated differently where the app clearly explains this.

Delete all locally stored Drive Simply data

You can remove all locally stored Drive Simply information by clearing the app's storage/data in your device settings or by uninstalling Drive Simply.

On Android, the exact path varies by manufacturer, but is usually similar to Settings → Apps → Drive Simply → Storage → Clear storage/data.

On iPhone, deleting the Drive Simply app removes the app and its locally stored application data from that device, subject to Apple's normal device and backup behaviour.

Exports

Deleting Drive Simply data does not delete copies of summaries, PDF reports or other exports that you previously saved, printed, emailed, uploaded or shared elsewhere. Those copies must be deleted from the destination where you stored or shared them.

Future accounts and cloud services

If Drive Simply introduces sign-in, cloud backup or account-based services, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated with the account-deletion process before those services are made available.
