Driving Safety

Effective: 31 August 2026 · Version 1.0

Set up Drive Simply before moving. Keep your phone secured or put away while driving. Only interact with it when safely parked.

Drive Simply is designed so that interaction during a drive is kept to a minimum. A learner, supervisor or other user must not use the app in a way that distracts from driving or breaches local road-traffic or mobile-phone law.

Phone use while driving

You are responsible for complying with the laws and rules that apply where you are driving. These can restrict holding, touching or interacting with a phone while driving, including while stopped in traffic.

Start the drive before setting off, place the phone securely away, and finish or edit the drive only when safely parked.

Supervision

Drive Simply does not determine whether a supervisor is legally entitled or appropriately insured to supervise a learner. The learner and supervisor are responsible for meeting all legal, licence, insurance and vehicle requirements that apply to them.

Not driving instruction

Drive Simply is a journal and reflection tool. It does not replace professional driving instruction, the Highway Code, official guidance, legal advice or an instructor's judgement.

Not an official record

A Drive Simply journal or PDF report is not issued, approved or certified by DVSA, DVLA or another licensing authority unless explicitly stated otherwise. It is not a guarantee of practical-test readiness or driving competence.

Location and distance

Distance, route and place information depends on device sensors, GPS availability, permissions and third-party operating-system services. Recorded values may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be relied upon for navigation, legal evidence or safety-critical decisions.

Emergency situations

Do not use Drive Simply as an emergency, breakdown or navigation service. In an emergency, follow the applicable emergency and roadside-safety guidance for your location.
