Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 August 2026 · Version 1.0

Drive Simply is a driving journal for learner drivers, provided by SPRK42 LTD.

Information used by Drive Simply

Drive Simply may store information you enter or create in the app, including learner names, supervisor names, vehicle details, transmission type, drive dates and times, drive duration, distance, driving conditions, road types, practised skills, vehicle-check markers, feelings and optional notes.

When you explicitly start a drive and grant location permission, Drive Simply uses device location to estimate distance and to build the journey record. This may include latitude/longitude samples and place names derived from those samples.

Where your journal is stored

In the current version, your Drive Simply journal is stored locally on your device. No Drive Simply account is required to create and use a journal.

If future versions introduce optional account, backup or synchronisation features, this policy will be updated before those services are enabled.

Location

Location is used only in connection with an explicitly started drive. On Android, Drive Simply may use a foreground location service so recording can continue while the screen is off. The operating system may show an ongoing location notification while a drive is active.

Location information is used to estimate journey distance and to create useful journey-place labels. If place-name lookup is unavailable, the drive can still be saved without those labels.

Exports and sharing

You may choose to copy a journal summary or, where available, create or share a report. Once you export or share information outside Drive Simply, the copy you created is controlled by the destination or recipient you selected.

Payments and Drive Simply Plus

Drive Simply may offer optional paid features such as Drive Simply Plus. Where purchases are available through Apple App Store or Google Play, payment processing is handled by the relevant store. SPRK42 LTD does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple or Google.

Drive Simply may receive purchase or subscription status information needed to provide paid features, restore purchases, prevent misuse and provide support. This policy will be updated if additional account or backend services are introduced.

What we do not do

We do not sell your journal or location data. We do not use your driving data for advertising. We do not track you across unrelated apps or services for advertising purposes.

Deleting your data

Because the current version stores the journal locally and does not require an account, you can remove data by deleting individual drives where the app provides that option, clearing the app's stored data, or uninstalling Drive Simply. See Delete Drive Simply Data for details.

Children and learner drivers

Drive Simply is intended to support learner-driver journalling. Where a user is under the age at which they can independently consent to applicable data processing, a parent or guardian should review use of the app and the device's privacy settings.

Security

We design Drive Simply to minimise unnecessary data collection. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for choosing where exported journal information is shared.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@sprk42.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Drive Simply evolves, including when account, cloud, analytics or payment-related services change. The current version will be published at this location.
