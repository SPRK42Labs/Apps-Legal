Draft: 14 September 2026 · Version 0.1

EDP Flow is a business workflow application used to coordinate customer orders, production work, homeworker activity, collections, deliveries and related operational records.

Authorised use

Access is for authorised users of the relevant EDP Flow deployment. You must use your own account, keep login details secure and only access information required for your role.

Operational records

EDP Flow records workflow information entered by users and generated as work moves through the system. Users are responsible for entering accurate information when accepting orders, allocating work, marking work ready, recording collections, deliveries and invoice references.

Customer orders and fulfilment

Order, allocation, collection, delivery and invoice quantities are tracked separately. The application is intended to support operational control and auditability, but users remain responsible for confirming commercial commitments, quantities, prices, tax treatment and invoices through the organisation's normal business processes.
