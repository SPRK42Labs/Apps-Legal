Delete EDP Flow Data

EDP Flow is a business workflow system. Some records are linked to customer orders, production activity, collections, deliveries, invoicing and audit history, so deleting an account does not necessarily mean every linked business record can or should be erased immediately.

Request account deletion

Email support@sprk42.com from the address associated with your account and state that you want your EDP Flow account deleted.

What happens next

We will verify the request, review the account's relationship to operational and business records, disable access where appropriate, and remove or anonymise personal data that is no longer required. Information that must be retained for legitimate business, accounting, contractual, security or audit purposes may be retained for the applicable period.

Customer and homeworker records

If your profile is connected to completed work, orders, deliveries or other auditable activity, the underlying operational record may need to remain while direct account access and unnecessary personal details are removed or restricted.

Questions

For questions about a deletion request, contact support@sprk42.com.

Final retention periods and controller responsibilities will be added before public-store publication.
