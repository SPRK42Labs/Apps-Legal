Privacy Notice

Draft: 14 September 2026 · Version 0.1

EDP Flow is a workflow application used to coordinate customer demand, products, production work, homeworker allocations, collections, deliveries and related operational records.

Information used by EDP Flow

Depending on your role, EDP Flow may process account and profile information, customer and contact details, product and pricing information, customer requests and orders, production quantities, homeworker allocations, collection and delivery dates, fulfilment progress, invoice references, operational notes and audit events.

Accounts and access

EDP Flow uses authenticated accounts with role-based access. Customers, homeworkers and administrators see different parts of the system according to the access granted to their account.

Where information is stored

EDP Flow uses cloud services to store application data and authentication records so authorised users can work across supported devices. The production deployment currently uses Firebase services supplied by Google.

Why information is used

Information is used to operate the workflow service, manage customer orders, allocate and track production work, record collections and deliveries, maintain an operational audit trail, support invoicing activity, administer user access and provide technical support.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that host or support the application. Access is limited to what is needed to provide the service. Deployment-specific controller and processor details must be confirmed before this notice is treated as final.

Retention and deletion

Operational records may need to be retained for business, accounting, contractual or audit purposes. Account or data deletion requests are reviewed in that context rather than automatically deleting every linked business record. See Delete EDP Flow Data.

Your responsibilities

Users should only enter personal or business information that is relevant to the workflow and should protect access to their account and device.

Contact

For privacy or data questions during the pilot, contact support@sprk42.com.

Before public publication

This draft must be updated with the confirmed EDP legal entity/data-controller identity, controller/processor responsibilities, retention periods and any additional subprocessors or analytics before public app-store publication.
