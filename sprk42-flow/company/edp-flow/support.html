Support

For help with EDP Flow during the pilot, contact support@sprk42.com.

When contacting support

Please include your name, your role (for example administrator, customer or homeworker), what you were trying to do, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

Account access

If you cannot sign in or your account appears linked to the wrong customer or homeworker profile, contact support so access can be checked.

Operational data

If an order, allocation, collection, delivery or invoice quantity looks wrong, stop before entering a compensating value and contact the administrator/support team so the audit trail can be reviewed.
